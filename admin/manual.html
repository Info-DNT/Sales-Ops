User Manual

Comprehensive guide to using the AirMedical 24X7 Portal

1. Authentication & Profile Management

1.1 Logging In

Navigate to the portal URL provided by your administrator. Enter your registered company email address and password. If you have forgotten your password or are a first-time user, contact your system administrator to issue a secure login credential.

1.2 User Details & Profile Setup

Once logged in, it is highly recommended to visit the User Details page from the left-hand navigation menu.

  • Your Information: Ensure your Full Name, Contact Number, and Email are accurate. This information is utilized when assigning leads.
  • Password Management: You can securely change your password from this screen. Regular password updates are encouraged for security purposes.

2. Dashboard Interface

The Home tab serves as your daily command center. Whenever you log in, this is the first screen you will see.

  • Analytical Widgets (KPI Tracker): At the top of the dashboard, you will find vital statistics updating in real-time. This includes your Total Leads, Converted Cases, Scheduled Meetings, and Total Expenses submitted.
  • Recent Activity Feed: This section provides a localized audit trail of your most recent actions (e.g., "Updated Lead Status," "Uploaded Expense Receipt"). Use this to quickly recap where you left off during your last session.
  • Navigation Menu: The left-side vertical menu provides quick access to every module in the system. The active module will always be highlighted in a distinct color.

3. Attendance Module

Note: Accurate time tracking is mandatory. The Attendance module logs your exact start and end times for the working day.

3.1 Clocking In & Out
  • Clock In: At the beginning of your shift, navigate to the Attendance tab and click the Clock In button. The system records your timestamp on the secure server.
  • Clock Out: At the end of your shift, return to this tab and click Clock Out. You cannot clock back in for the same calendar day.
3.2 Attendance History

Below the action buttons is your Attendance History Table. It displays a chronological list of your workdays including exact Check-in Time, Check-out Time, Total Calculated Hours, and Daily Status.

4. Leads Management

The Leads tab is the starting point for all prospective business. A "Lead" represents a potential client who has not yet financially committed.

4.1 Creating a New Lead

Click the + Add Lead button (top right) to open the Lead Creation Form.

  • Client vs. Patient: Accurately distinguish the "Client" (the person paying or organizing the transport) from the "Patient" (the person needing transport).
  • Travel Details: Provide accurate Source and Destination locations, as these are critical for generating flight quotations.
  • CRM Syncing: Once you click "Save Lead", the system securely bridges with our corporate Zoho CRM. This generates a unique Serial No. for tracking.
4.2 Tracking & Updating Leads
  • Filtering & Searching: Use the top bar to search by phone number or filter by specific statuses (New, In Progress, Qualified).
  • Lead Actions: Click the "Eye/Details" icon to open the Details panel where you can view a chronological timeline of interactions.
4.3 Converting to a Case

When a Lead agrees to proceed with an invoice/transport, change their status to Converted. This officially locks the lead from generic edits and automatically moves it to the Cases tab.

5. Case & Document Management (Crucial Workflow)

The Cases tab operates on a Strict Sequential Document Workflow. You manage these steps via the "Documents" dropdown menu on each Case card.

Important: You cannot skip steps. A button will only unlock once the prerequisite step is completed by you and the accounts team.

  • Quotation Phase: Click Request Quotation to alert the pricing team. Once approved, click Download Quotation to fetch the PDF directly from Google Drive to send to your client.
  • Proforma Phase: Click Request Proforma. When accounts uploads it, click Download Proforma. Automatically, clicking download will permanently unlock the Tax Invoice options.
  • Tax Invoice Phase: Click Request Tax Invoice. Once accounts uploads it, click Download Tax Invoice. Automatically, clicking download will permanently unlock the Receipt options.
  • Receipt Phase: Once full payment is secured, request and download the finalized payment Receipt to close the financial loop.

If you click 'Download' before the Accounts team has uploaded the corresponding PDF to the central Google Drive, the system will prevent the download and alert you with: "Pending request from accounts team."

6. Daily Activities: Calls & Meetings

6.1 Logging Calls

Navigate to the Calls tab to record phone interactions.

  • Identify the client and log the duration.
  • Summarize the talking points.
  • Set a specific "Follow-up Action" to ensure no communication is forgotten.
6.2 Managing Meetings

Navigate to the Meetings tab to schedule physical or virtual appointments.

  • Add meeting locations, expected durations, and attendees (e.g., Hospital Administration).
  • Use this to organize your upcoming week and review past meeting outcomes.

7. Expense Management

The Expenses tab allows you to submit reimbursement requests for business-related spending.

  • Adding an Expense: Click "+ Add Expense" and fill in the exact amount, date, and category.
  • Receipt Uploads: You must attach an image or PDF of the receipt for accounting validation.

8. Daily Work Report

The Work Report tab is your mandatory end-of-day summary submission.

  • Provide a detailed summary of key tasks accomplished and important client updates.
  • List any challenges faced during the day.
  • Outline your high-priority goals for the following day.
