Admin Manual

Administrative guide for managing the AirMedical 24X7 Portal

1. Authentication & Security

1.1 Admin Access

Admins have full access to all system modules. Ensure your credentials are secure and multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enabled if applicable through the identity provider.

1.2 Session Management

The system automatically manages active sessions. If you remain inactive for an extended period, you may be prompted to log in again to maintain security.

2. Admin Dashboard & Analytics

The Admin Home tab provides a bird's-eye view of the entire organization's performance.

  • Global KPIs: Monitor total revenue, pending invoices, and active leads across the entire team.
  • Recent Activity Feed: Audit log of actions taken by all users in the system.
  • Quick Quotation: Accessible at the top right to quickly generate pricing for urgent requests.

3. User & Staff Management

As an Administrator, you are responsible for onboarding and managing staff accounts via the Users tab.

  • Creating Users: Add new staff members by providing their email and setting their initial role (User/Admin).
  • Editing Access: You can update user profile details, change roles, or deactivate accounts if a staff member leaves the organization.
  • Password Resets: Admins can trigger password resets for staff members who are unable to access their accounts.

4. Global Leads Management

4.1 Oversight

Admins can view and edit all leads regardless of who they are assigned to. This is crucial for load balancing and quality control.

4.2 Lead Actions & Assessments
  • Medical Assessment: Review patient clinical data submitted via the Zoho assessment forms.
  • Request Quotation: Initiate or review pricing requests for any lead.
  • CRM Integration: Monitor the synchronization status with Zoho CRM to ensure data consistency.

5. Global Case Workflow

Manage the lifecycle of all active transports. Admins have the authority to bypass certain restrictions if necessary, though following the standard sequence is recommended.

Accounts Coordination: Admins can oversee the "Quotation → Proforma → Tax Invoice → Receipt" flow and coordinate with the accounting team for file uploads.

  • Quotation: Review and download approved quotations.
  • Proforma: Monitor proforma issuance and payment status.
  • Tax Invoice: Finalize billing and ensure tax invoices are correctly uploaded to Google Drive.

6. Reporting & Analytics

The Reports tab allows you to generate comprehensive data exports for business analysis.

  • Performance Reports: Export lead conversion rates and staff activity logs.
  • Financial Summaries: Generate reports on expenses and invoice statuses for the accounting department.
  • Attendance Logs: Download detailed check-in/out logs for payroll processing.

7. Expense Approval Workflow

Review and approve/reject expense submissions from all staff members via the Expenses tab.

  • Validation: Check the attached receipt images against the submitted amounts.
  • Approval: Marking an expense as "Approved" alerts the staff member and moves it to the payout queue.
