# AirMedical 24X7 Sales Team Portal - Comprehensive User Manual 

Welcome to the AirMedical 24X7 Sales Team Portal. This application is your central hub for managing client relationships, tracking medical transport requests, managing critical documentation, and logging your daily activities. 

This manual provides an in-depth breakdown of every section of the application to ensure you can utilize the system effectively and efficiently.

---

## 1. Authentication & Profile Management

### 1.1 Logging In
Navigate to the portal URL provided by your administrator. Enter your registered company email address and password. If you have forgotten your password or are a first-time user, contact your system administrator to issue a secure login credential.

### 1.2 User Details & Profile Setup
Once logged in, it is highly recommended to visit the **User Details** page from the left-hand navigation menu.
- **Your Information:** Ensure your Full Name, Contact Number, and Email are accurate. This information is often utilized when generating automated communications or assigning leads.
- **Password Management:** You can securely change your password from this screen. Regular password updates are encouraged for security purposes.

---

## 2. Dashboard Interface

The **Home** tab serves as your daily command center. Whenever you log in, this is the first screen you will see.

- **Analytical Widgets (KPI Tracker):** At the top of the dashboard, you will find vital statistics updating in real-time. This includes your Total Leads, Converted Cases, Scheduled Meetings, and Total Expenses submitted.
- **Recent Activity Feed:** This section provides a localized audit trail of your most recent actions (e.g., "Updated Lead Status," "Uploaded Expense Receipt"). Use this to quickly recap where you left off during your last session.
- **Navigation Menu:** The left-side vertical menu provides quick access to every module in the system. The active module will always be highlighted in a distinct color.

---

## 3. Attendance Module

Accurate time tracking is mandatory. The **Attendance** module logs your exact start and end times for the working day.

### 3.1 Clocking In & Out
- **Clock In:** At the beginning of your shift, navigate to the Attendance tab and click the **Clock In** button. The system records your timestamp on the secure server.
- **Clock Out:** At the end of your shift, return to this tab and click **Clock Out**. Once configured, you cannot clock back in for the same calendar day.

### 3.2 Attendance History
Below the action buttons is your **Attendance History Table**. 
- It displays a chronological list of your workdays. 
- You can view your exact Check-in Time, Check-out Time, Total Calculated Hours, and your Daily Status (e.g., Present, Half-day, Absent).

---

## 4. Leads Management

The **Leads** tab is the starting point for all prospective business. A "Lead" represents a potential medical transport or tourism client who has not yet financially committed.

### 4.1 Creating a New Lead
Click the **+ Add Lead** button (top right) to open the Lead Creation Form. 
- **Client vs. Patient:** Accurately distinguish the "Client" (the person paying or organizing the transport, such as a family member or insurance agent) from the "Patient" (the person needing transport).
- **Travel Details:** Provide accurate Source and Destination locations, as these are critical for generating flight quotations.
- **Lead Source:** Indicate where the lead originated (WhatsApp, Hospital Reference, Embassy, etc.) for marketing analytics.
- **CRM Syncing:** Once you click "Save Lead", the system securely bridges with our corporate Zoho CRM. This generates a unique **Serial No.** for tracking.

### 4.2 Tracking & Updating Leads
Every lead appears as a detailed card on your dashboard.
- **Filtering & Searching:** Use the top bar to search by phone number, date, or filter by specific statuses (New, In Progress, Qualified).
- **Lead Actions:** Click the small "Eye/Details" icon to open the Lead Details panel. Here you can view a chronological timeline of interactions.
- **Updating Status:** As you work with a client, continually update their status. If a lead goes cold, mark it as "Not Converted." If a lead agrees to proceed with an invoice/transport, mark them as **Converted**.

### 4.3 Converting to a Case
When a Lead's status is changed to **Converted**, they officially become pending business. The lead is locked from further generic edits and is automatically moved to the **Cases** tab.

## 5. Case & Document Management (The Workflow)

The **Cases** tab contains your confirmed clients. Because medical transport involves significant financial and legal documentation, this section operates on a **Strict Sequential Document Workflow**. You manage these steps via the "Documents" dropdown menu on each Case card.

You **cannot** skip steps. A button will only unlock once the prerequisite step is completed by you and the accounts team.

### Step 1: Quotation Phase
1. **Request Quotation:** Generates a formal request alerting the backend pricing team to calculate flight costs based on the initial lead parameters.
2. **Download Quotation:** Once the Quotation ID is generated and the file is approved in the central Google Drive, this button activates. Clicking it fetches the PDF directly to your browser for you to send to the client.

### Step 2: Proforma Phase
1. **Request Proforma:** After the client agrees to the quotation price, click this button to request an official Proforma Invoice.
2. **Download Proforma** When the accounts team uploads the Proforma to the secure Drive, clicking this fetches the document. 
   - *Crucial automated step:* The moment you successfully download the Proforma, the system registers the action and immediately **Unlocks the Final Invoice options.**

### Step 3: Final Invoice Phase
1. **Request Final Invoice:** Now unlocked, you may submit this request to accounts (usually triggered upon receiving patient fit-to-fly confirmation or initial deposit).
2. **Download Final Invoice:** Fetches the finalized invoice PDF. 
   - *Crucial automated step:* Downloading the Final Invoice instantly **Unlocks the Receipt options.**

### Step 4: Receipt Phase
1. **Request Receipt / Download Receipt:** Once full payment is secured, request and download the finalized payment receipt to close the financial loop.

*Note on Missing Files:* If you attempt to click any "Download" button before the Accounts team has uploaded the corresponding PDF to the central Google Drive, the system will prevent the download and alert you with a message: **"Pending request from accounts team."**

## 6. Daily Activities: Calls & Meetings

Maintaining a robust activity log is essential for team accountability and client history tracking.

### 6.1 Logging Calls
Navigate to the **Calls** tab to record phone interactions.
- **New Call Log:** Identify the client, log the duration of the call, summarize the talking points, and set a specific "Follow-up Action."
- This creates a searchable database ensuring no client communication is forgotten.

### 6.2 Managing Meetings
Navigate to the **Meetings** tab to schedule and review physical or virtual appointments.
- Add meeting locations, expected durations, and attendees (e.g., Hospital Administration, Client Family).
- Use this to organize your upcoming week and review past meeting outcomes.

---

## 7. Expense Management

The **Expenses** tab allows you to submit reimbursement requests for business-related spending (e.g., travel, client dining, medical report collection).

- **Adding an Expense:** Click "+ Add Expense" and fill in the exact amount, date, and category (Travel, Meals, Miscellaneous).
- **Receipt Uploads:** You must attach an image or PDF of the receipt for accounting validation.
- **Expense History:** The main table tracks all your submitted expenses. Contact the finance department regarding the approval status of specific submissions.

---

## 8. Daily Work Report

The **Work Report** tab is your end-of-day summary submission. Management relies on these reports to gauge daily team productivity and assist with roadblocks.

- **Filing a Report:** At the conclusion of your shift, navigate here. Provide a detailed summary of:
  - Key tasks accomplished.
  - Important client updates.
  - Any challenges or issues faced during the day.
  - Your high-priority goals for the following day.
- Consistent, detailed work reports are a mandatory metric for performance reviews.
